Model Architecture & Pipeline

Fine-tuned sequence classification pipeline specifications.

01

WordPiece Tokenizer

Transforms raw unicode text into subword token IDs. Max sequence length: 256. Vocabulary size: 30,522 tokens.

02

DistilBERT Transformer Encoder

66.3M parameters across 6 Transformer encoder blocks with 12 multi-head self-attention mechanisms.

03

Pooled Representation

Extracts the 768-dimensional contextual hidden state vector from the special [CLS] classification token.

04

Classification Head & Softmax

Applies 0.2 dropout, dense linear layer (768 → 2 logits), and softmax normalization to produce final probabilities.

Hardware Acceleration: Apple Silicon (MPS)
